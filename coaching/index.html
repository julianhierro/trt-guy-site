What's included with my coaching?

If your goal is to develop a strong, healthy, aesthetic and athletic physique, then these are the four pillars you need to include in planning. Each of these pillars is a dial. And depending on what you want to accomplish, you can turn up or turn down each of them.

01

Strength

Strength training should be one of the foundations of your program. You do not need dozens of complicated exercises. You need to get very good at the basics, execute them well, and progressively improve over time.

02

Cardiovascular capacity

You can be as strong as a bull, but if you get winded going up a flight of stairs, it’s pretty useless. That’s why you also need to be fit.

03

Favorable body composition

How much muscle are you carrying, and how much body fat are you carrying? Your ideal body composition depends on your current condition and your goals. Nutrition moves this dial.

04

Hormones, peptides & performance support

The internal environment that can support your training, recovery, health, and performance. We make all decisions together based on your blood work, your current health status, and the level of risk you want to take. We can be super conservative, or if you want to push things, I can help you do that too while we preserve your health as much as possible.

All plans include the pillars, but we dial each one up or down depending on what you need.
