How I restored my fertility

My experience

After years of PED use, my sperm count was zero.

I used a fertility-restoration protocol very similar to the one in this guide, brought my sperm back, and my wife and I conceived our first daughter without having to come off steroids (I was using higher doses back then).

This worked for me, and I want men in a similar position to understand the approach instead of automatically assuming their fertility is permanently gone.

Before you start

Get a formal semen analysis before starting. You need to know where things are at, and this will allow you to compare and decide if the protocol is working.

Should you administer HCG and HMG on the same day or alternating days?

There is no established fertility advantage to alternating hCG on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday and hMG on Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday. Using both on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday reduces the number of injection days and is commonly done. Keep them in separate syringes unless the prescriber or dispensing pharmacy specifically confirms they may be mixed.

Do not crash yourself while trying to conceive

TRT, PEDs, and sexual function

You need sperm to conceive, but you also need libido, erections, energy, and a healthy relationship. In my opinion, deliberately crashing your testosterone and feeling terrible is not the ideal environment for trying to make a baby.

I didn’t have to stop taking PEDs to conceive my first daughter, and I started with a sperm count of zero.

Removing TRT/PEDs will bring other issues since you will also experience hypogonadal symptoms which will make it harder to conceive.

Do not make yourself hypogonadal just because you are trying to conceive. Keep the androgen side as simple as possible and measure whether sperm is actually returning.

Eat in a slight calorie surplus

This is not the time for an aggressive fat-loss phase. Sperm production is a resource-intensive biological process. I would rather give the body slightly more energy than it needs than ask it to restore fertility while dieting hard.

Increase your chances of conception

Have sex often

A protocol can improve the semen analysis, but conception still requires frequent intercourse.

My practical target is sex every day while trying to conceive. During the fertile window, once or twice per day is an option if both partners feel good and it does not turn the process into a chore.

Use ovulation strips or another reliable method to identify the fertile window. The highest-priority days are the two days before ovulation and the day of ovulation.

Other basics that still matter

  • Sleep enough to recover and maintain sexual function.
  • Avoid smoking, recreational drugs, excessive alcohol, and frequent high-heat exposure to the testes.
  • Keep moderate training in, but do not combine hard dieting, excessive cardio, poor sleep, and maximal training volume.
  • If erections, ejaculation, or desire are limiting intercourse, address the cause instead of pretending the problem is only motivation.
